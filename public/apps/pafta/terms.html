4. Safety and limits of the app

Pafta is a navigation aid. It doesn't replace your own judgment, training, a paper map, a compass or official information. You are responsible for planning your trip and for your own safety and the safety of others.

  • Position. GPS accuracy depends on your device, the sky view, terrain, buildings, weather and other conditions. The position shown can be off by tens of meters or more, or be unavailable.
  • Maps. Maps come from third-party and community sources such as OpenStreetMap. They can be outdated, incomplete or wrong. A path, road or crossing shown on the map may not exist, may be closed or may not be legal or safe to use.
  • Elevation. Elevation data is a surface model (it includes tree canopy and buildings) and can differ from the actual ground height. Slopes, ascent figures and profiles are estimates.
  • Weather and sun times. Forecasts can be wrong, and conditions in the mountains can change quickly. Sun and moon times are calculated and may differ from local conditions.
  • Alerts and recording. Off-route, arrival and sunset alerts, and track recording, depend on your device's battery, settings and operating system. They can be delayed, missed or stopped, for example by battery saving or when a permission is turned off.
  • Coordinates and printed maps. Check coordinates before you rely on them in critical situations, and make sure the coordinate system and datum match the one used by others. The scale of a printed map depends on your printer settings; print at actual size and check the scale bar.
  • SOS. The SOS screen doesn't contact emergency services or anyone else by itself. It prepares a message that you send yourself, and delivery depends on your phone, its mobile network and the messaging app you use. Where possible, call your local emergency number.
  • Not for every use. The app isn't designed or certified for aviation or marine navigation, for military or other professional use that requires certified equipment, or as the only means of navigation where an error could cause injury, death or damage.
  • Laws and access. Follow local laws, signs and closures, and respect restricted and military areas and private property. Showing a place on a map doesn't mean you may enter it.
