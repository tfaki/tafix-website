2. Driving safety

Your safety is your responsibility. ZEF helps you plan around the weather. It doesn't replace official weather warnings, road authorities, emergency services or your own judgment, and it can't see the road in front of you.

  • Pay attention to the road. Don't read, type or tap on your phone while driving. Plan your route before you set off, or have a passenger use the app. Follow all laws about using phones and navigation devices in vehicles.
  • Forecasts aren't certain. Weather data, radar images and official alerts may be wrong, late, incomplete or unavailable. Risk levels (Low, Caution, High, Severe) are estimates based on forecasts. “Low”, “Clear” or no warning doesn't mean the road is safe.
  • The app doesn't show road conditions. Ice, flooding, closures, accidents, construction and other hazards may not be reflected in the app.
  • Routes are for regular vehicles. Routes come from Apple or Google and are calculated for passenger vehicles. Vehicle profiles (such as truck, RV or motorcycle) only change how weather risk is scored. They don't consider your vehicle's height, weight, length or cargo, or road restrictions. Always follow posted signs and road restrictions.
  • Alerts can be missed. Voice alerts and notifications depend on your device's location, internet connection, volume, battery and permission settings. Don't rely on them as your only warning.
  • Coverage varies. Radar is available only in some regions. Official alerts depend on what each country's weather services provide, and some countries have none in the app.
  • Not for other uses. Don't use ZEF for emergencies or for aviation, marine or other purposes where an error could cause serious harm.
