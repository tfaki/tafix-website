What Inviso does

Inviso opens electronic invoices, signed documents and related files that you already have and shows them in readable form on your device. It does not send or submit invoices to anyone, and it does not connect to the Italian SdI or Peppol. If you choose to connect it to KSeF, the Polish National e-Invoice System, it downloads the invoices you received there to your device; it never sends or changes anything in KSeF.
