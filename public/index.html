Which devices are the apps made for?

Our collection includes apps for iOS, Android and Wear OS. Check the platform label on each app card and the app’s store listing for device compatibility.

Where can I check an app’s price?

Pricing and available purchases are shown in each app’s store listing and purchase screen. They can vary by app, platform and region.

How do I get help with an app?

Email support@tafix.co with the app name, your device model and a short description of the issue. A screenshot is helpful too.

Can I suggest a feature?

Absolutely. Tell us what you would like to do and which app you are using. Send your idea to support@tafix.co.

Where can I find privacy information?

Each app has its own privacy information, available through its store listing or within the app. If you need help finding it, contact us with the app name.
