Open P7M files and electronic invoices

Drop a .p7m, .p7s, XML, PDF, ZIP or email file below. Inviso unwraps the signed envelope and shows the invoice as a readable document.

Your file never leaves your device. It is opened by your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Choose the file or drag it onto the box above.
  2. Inviso removes the digital signature envelope (CAdES) and finds the invoice inside, also in ZIP archives and certified emails (PEC).
  3. Read the invoice: seller and buyer, lines, VAT summary, totals and payment details, with an automatic check of the totals.

Supported files

  • P7M in DER, Base64 and PEM, also nested (.p7m.p7m); P7S, TSD and M7M
  • Italian FatturaPA (FPR12, FPA12, simplified FSM10) and SdI notifications
  • UBL 2.1 and Peppol BIS 3, XRechnung (UBL and CII)
  • ZUGFeRD and Factur-X PDFs with embedded XML
  • Polish KSeF FA(2) and FA(3), Spanish Facturae 3.2
  • ZIP and ASiC archives, EML and PEC emails, Outlook MSG

Frequently asked questions

What is a P7M file?

A P7M file is a document with a digital signature in CAdES format (PKCS #7). The original file, usually an XML invoice or a PDF, is inside the signed envelope. In Italy, electronic invoices signed by the supplier arrive as .xml.p7m files.

Is it safe to open my invoice here?

Yes. The file is read by your browser on your own device and is not uploaded anywhere. The page is not allowed to send it to another server.

Is the digital signature verified?

The web viewer shows who signed the file. Checking the signature, the certificate and the EU trust list is done in the Inviso app.

Which e-invoice formats can I read?

FatturaPA, UBL and Peppol, XRechnung, ZUGFeRD and Factur-X, KSeF and Facturae. Other XML files are shown as a structured tree.

Can I save the invoice as a PDF?

Saving as PDF, printing and exporting the lines to a spreadsheet are in the Inviso app. In the browser you can view one file at a time.

Does it cost anything?

The web viewer is free and needs no account.
